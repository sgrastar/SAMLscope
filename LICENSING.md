# Licensing scope

The accompanying `LICENSE` contains the Apache License, Version 2.0, for
SAMLscope's original software implementation. It does not relicense third-party
libraries, specification text, schemas, or other third-party material bundled
with the application.

Dependency JARs retain their own license and notice resources. Their terms apply
to their respective contents. Keep those JAR resources when redistributing the
application; the application's Apache license does not replace them.

The requirement catalog, test definitions, profile mappings and explanatory
content contain material derived from external specifications. Their source
licenses and required notices must be preserved separately. The intended policy
of CC BY-SA 4.0 for eligible original prose and definitions does not override the
terms of incorporated third-party material. Measured results are not assigned a
Creative Commons license merely because they are exported by this software.

The material-specific attribution review is still in progress. The application
and distributions expose the notices currently retained in
`LICENSES/source-notices.json`, and browser bundles retain the license text of
their included packages. Registered unresolved sources remain visible in that
registry. This does not establish that every specification, schema or standalone
result output has completed material-level review.

`result.json` records immutable source and definition references used for the
assessment. The online result view links to the application notice registry.
Standalone `report.html` embeds this scope statement, the Apache-2.0 text and the
retained source-notice registry. The registry remains explicit about unresolved
sources; embedding it does not complete their material-level review.
